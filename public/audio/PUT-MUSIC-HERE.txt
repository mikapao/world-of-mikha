Background music for World of Mikha
===================================

Put ONE music file in this folder named exactly:

    bgm.mp3

It plays on a loop during the game, softly, starting when Mikha taps ▶ Play.
(No file here = no music; the game works fine without it.)

Tips for a good BGM file
- Calm, gentle instrumental music (no lyrics) – music box, soft piano, ukulele, lullaby-style.
- 1–3 minutes long, made to loop smoothly (the end flows into the start).
- MP3, 128 kbps, stereo – about 1–3 MB. Keep it under 5 MB so the app stays small.
- Use music you're allowed to use: royalty-free / Creative Commons music, or a track you bought
  a licence for. Check the licence says it's OK to use in an app.

Volume
- Grown-ups can change the music volume (or turn it off) in the parent corner (hold ⚙︎).
- The music fades out completely when Mikha enters a challenge and comes back
  when she leaves or finishes it (so voices and the microphone are clear).

To change the song later: replace bgm.mp3 with a new file of the same name, then push to GitHub.
