## Acceptance criteria
1. Two presses within 300 ms and 40 px make one hop and one soft sound, in avatar mode (press within reach) and in pad mode.
2. A single tap, a hold-and-slide, and pad walking behave exactly as before (the detector only observes).
3. A hop in progress is not restarted. A triple tap gives one hop.
4. No hop while a UI is open (`blocked()`).
5. `setAvatar` still hops silently, as before.
6. `npm run build` passes.
