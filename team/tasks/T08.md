# T08 — Land animals
Design: `team/02_design.md` D6 (land part). Model: Sonnet (Opus on a second attempt). Status: done (2026-11-22)

## Files this task may touch
- `src/world/animals.js` (new), `src/main.js` (two call sites)

## Acceptance criteria
1. Roster per D6 on all 5 islands, original simple designs, ≤ 300 triangles each.
2. They never stand within 2.8 of a monument ring or 2.0 of a bridge end, and never inside an obstacle circle. They stay inside the walk circle minus 1.0.
3. They don't block the avatar, make no sound, and are culled beyond 22 units from the camera target.
4. `npm run build` passes.
