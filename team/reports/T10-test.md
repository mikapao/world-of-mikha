- **What I did:** ran the T10 test file. It searches the collector output (`scripts/voice-lines.mjs`, the same one CI uses) for the sentence "Yes!".
- **Expected:** a clip for "Yes!", because `src/monuments/lollipop.js` line 95 says `ctx.speak(\`Yes! ${q.word}!\`)` after each right answer, and the word part is covered by the vocab rule.
- **Actual:** no clip. The "Yes!" sentence was in `FIXED_LINES` at HEAD (`src/voiceLines.js` line 12, `"Yes!"`). So after the next cloud build the Lollipop's "Yes!" is spoken by the tablet voice, not Alice, and sounds different from the rest.
- **Where:** `src/voiceLines.js` (FIXED_LINES, no "Yes!" entry) and `src/monuments/lollipop.js` line 95. Fix is for the Coder: add `"Yes!"` to `FIXED_LINES` (or change the Lollipop line to a listed sentence, e.g. "Yes, well done!"), then re-run this report.
